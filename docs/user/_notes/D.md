# Agent D notes for the user manual (security, users, audit, data protection)

## Signing in
- Passwords: at least 12 characters. A short sentence or 3–4 words works well ("mango tree by the lagoon"). No symbols required. Common or leaked passwords are refused. Passwords never expire.
- 5 wrong attempts lock the account for 15 minutes (longer if it keeps happening). The administrator can unlock it (Users → Unlock).
- ADMIN and BURSAR must set up an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) at first sign-in. Save the 10 recovery codes on paper; each works once if the phone is lost.
- The session ends after 30 minutes without activity, and after 12 hours in any case.
- New users get a temporary password from the administrator and must change it at first sign-in.
- "Your page is out of date. Please refresh" means the security token expired: refresh the page.

## Users (ADMIN)
- Create user → the screen shows a one-time temporary password to give the person.
- Changing someone's roles or turning their account off signs them out everywhere at once.
- Force logout, Unlock, Reset two-step sign-in (new phone), Reset password (gives a new temporary password).
- You cannot change your own roles, and the school must keep at least one active administrator.

## Audit log (ADMIN, HEADTEACHER)
- Shows who did what and when, including who opened a child's Emergency View or health record. Filter by student to answer "who looked at my child's record?".
- "Verify" checks that nobody has altered the log. Anything other than "intact" must be reported to the proprietor at once (see breach procedure).

## Data protection (ADMIN; REGISTRAR can log requests; HEADTEACHER can view)
- Requests: log every parent request to see, correct, delete or stop use of data. Due date = 21 days (shown in red when overdue).
- Subject access export: gathers everything about one student. Check it and remove other people's information before handing it over.
- Retention candidates: former students past the retention period (default 7 years after leaving).
- Anonymise: irreversible; type the student code to confirm. Keeps the student code and fee records only.
