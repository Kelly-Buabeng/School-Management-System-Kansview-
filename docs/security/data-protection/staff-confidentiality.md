# Staff confidentiality rules

Every staff member signs these rules before the administrator creates their account. Keep the signed copy in the staff file. Plain language on purpose.

## The rules

1. **Children's information is confidential.** Use it only for your work at the school. Do not discuss a child's health, family situation, discipline or fees with people who do not need to know, including other parents, other staff, or friends.
2. **Your account is yours alone.** Never share your password or your authenticator phone. Never sign in for someone else. If you think someone knows your password, change it and tell the administrator.
3. **Lock or sign out** when you leave a computer, even for a few minutes. The system signs you out after 30 minutes of no activity, but do not rely on it.
4. **Look only at what you need.** Every time you open a child's record, Emergency View or health details, the system records your name and the time. The head reviews these records. Looking up children out of curiosity is misuse.
5. **No copies on personal phones or WhatsApp.** Do not photograph screens, forward class lists, or post children's names, photos or marks in chat groups. Use the system's messaging for parents.
6. **Exports and printouts.** Only export or print when needed for school work. Store printouts in a locked drawer and shred them when finished. Exports are recorded.
7. **Ghana Card and health details.** Only the administrator can reveal a full Ghana Card number, and must give a reason. Health details are for keeping the child safe; never mention them in front of other children.
8. **Phone calls asking for information.** Do not give a child's details, location or timetable to callers, even if they say they are a parent. Ask them to come to the office with identification, and check the pickup list.
9. **Report problems at once.** If you lose a device, send information to the wrong person, or see something suspicious, tell the head or administrator immediately. Reporting quickly is never punished; hiding a mistake is.
10. **When you leave the school** your account is closed. Do not keep any school data.

Breaking these rules may lead to disciplinary action, and misuse of personal data can be an offence under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). **[CONFIRM]** the disciplinary wording with the school's employment adviser.

## For the administrator

- Give each person only the role they need. TEACHER accounts see only their assigned classes; check class assignments at the start of each year.
- ADMIN and BURSAR must set up the authenticator app at first sign-in (the system enforces it).
- When someone leaves: **Users → [user] → Edit → Status: disabled**, then **Force logout**.
- Review the user list each term (last sign-in date column) and disable unused accounts.
