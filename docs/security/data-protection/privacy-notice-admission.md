# Privacy notice for the admission form

Print this on the back of the admission form or hand it to the parent with the form. Keep the language plain. Fill in the parts in _italics_. **[CONFIRM]** the final text with a lawyer before printing.

---

## How Kansview School uses your child's information

**Who we are.** _Kansview School, [address], [phone], [email]._ We are responsible for the information on this form. Our data protection lead is _[name and phone]_.

**What we collect.** Your child's name, date of birth, photo, address, previous school and birth certificate number; your name, phone numbers, address, work details and (if you give it) your Ghana Card number; your child's health information such as allergies, medical conditions and medication; and, while your child is with us, attendance, marks, report cards, fees and payments.

**Why we collect it.**

- to admit and teach your child, keep registers and write report cards;
- to keep your child safe: to know who may collect your child, whom to call in an emergency and what to do if your child is ill or has an allergy;
- to send you messages about your child (absence, fees, school closures);
- to manage fees and keep accounting records the law requires.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

**Health information.** We ask about health so staff can act quickly in an emergency. Only the head, the administrator and the front office can see the full details. Class teachers see a short alert (for example "Severe peanut allergy"). The details are stored in coded (encrypted) form.

**Who else sees it.** Our SMS company receives your phone number and the text of messages we send you. Our backup company stores coded copies we cannot lose. If your child moves school, we send the new school a transfer letter. We share information with government bodies only when the law requires it.

**How long we keep it.** We keep your child's records while they are with us and for _7 years_ after they leave. After that we remove names, addresses, health and other personal details, and keep only the student number and fee records that the law requires us to keep.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see the information we hold about your child and you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete information we no longer need, or object to a use of it. Speak to the data protection lead or write to the school. We will answer within _21 days_. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the **Data Protection Commission** of Ghana.

**Keeping it safe.** Only staff who need the information can see it. Every time someone opens your child's emergency details or health record, the system records who did it.

**Photos for publicity.** We will **not** use your child's photo on our website, social media or posters unless you sign a separate consent form.

I have read this notice. Parent/guardian name: ____________________ Signature: ____________ Date: __________
